Work / Interests

Through years of training and competition, I earned a state championship title, which reflects my dedication, consistency, and competitive mindset. Beyond fitness, I am also focused on building my own personal brand called Calus and Trade. This brand represents my passion for entrepreneurship, self-improvement, and financial growth. I’m interested in business, branding, content creation, and trading, and I enjoy learning how to turn ideas into something meaningful and impactful. Whether it’s in the gym, at work, or through my own projects, I’m always looking for ways to grow, challenge myself, and build a future based on hard work and ambition.
