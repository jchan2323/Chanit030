About Me

My name is Justin Chan, I am 19 years old studying at Hofstra University. I am from Long Island, New York. I have an interest in technology and web development. I’m passionate about self-improvement, creativity, and pushing myself to grow in different areas of life. One of my biggest interests is powerlifting, where I enjoy challenging myself both mentally and physically through discipline, consistency, and hard work. Fitness has taught me the importance of dedication and staying committed to long-term goals. Outside of the gym, I have a strong passion for cars and automotive culture. I enjoy learning about different vehicles, performance builds, and the creativity that goes into making every car unique. Cars represent more than transportation to me they reflect individuality, innovation, and personal style. I also enjoy hiking and spending time outdoors. Exploring new places and being surrounded by nature gives me a chance to recharge, stay active, and appreciate new experiences outside of everyday routines. Alongside my hobbies and interests, I’m currently working on building my own brand. Creating something of my own is an exciting journey that allows me to combine my creativity, ambition, and personal vision into something meaningful. My goal is to inspire others while building a brand that represents authenticity, motivation, and growth.
